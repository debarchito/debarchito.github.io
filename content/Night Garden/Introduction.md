### 🌙 Riddles aside, what is it really about?

Night Garden is an open diary sharing perspectives, fostering exploration,
contemplation and introspection; a place for me to speak my heart out about my
takes on various topics. It is foremost a way to preserve my thoughts,
reflections, and insights for myself. You are invited to step into this journey
alongside me, where events unfurl for you to judge as we wander through the
plains.
