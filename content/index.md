> `d'DG` (or `ddg` for URI safe) — a **digital garden** that sprouted on **March
> 26th, 2024**. The name is a simple abbreviation: **d**ebarchito's **D**igital
> **G**arden.

I'm **Debarchito Nath** (also known online as `Andew`). I'm a computer science
student who loves math a bit too much. I've been writing stories, poems, and
other literary works for a fairly long time, but I've kept them to myself for
the most part. But, its about time, I change that. Through `d'DG`, I plan to
slowly but steadily share my creations with the world as well as create a unique
digital place for myself. My socials (non-exhaustive):
