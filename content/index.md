> `d'DG` (or `ddg` for URI safe) is a simple abbreviation: **d**ebarchito's
> **D**igital **G**arden.

Yahallo! I'm **debarchito**, a computer science student who likes a good type
system. I'm currently researching and building
[Miru](https://git.sr.ht/~debarchito/miru), a strictly evaluated functional
language that borrows much of its syntax from languages like
[Clojure](https://clojure.org),
[Carp](https://carp-lang.github.io/Carp/LanguageGuide.html), and
[Shen](https://shen-language.github.io) while implementing the semantics of
languages like [OCaml](https://ocaml.org), [Haskell](https://haskell.org), and
[Koka](https://koka-lang.github.io/koka/doc/index.html). with great premise on
_Algebraic Effects_ and _Effect Tracking_
([WikiPedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effect_system) for your reference).
It additionally provides native array-language extensions including contiguous
_N-dimensional tensors_ and _broadcasting_ inspired by
[Julia](https://julialang.org). It's designed to be pragmatic and useful for day
to day general purpose tasks while also being a great fit for doing math and
science. A great way to get an overview of the language is to read the
[DRAFT](https://git.sr.ht/~debarchito/miru/tree/main/item/DRAFT.md)
specification.

Conversely, I like building things that solve my day to day chores. Good
examples being [minework](https://git.sr.ht/~debarchito/minework) to manage
everything Minecraft, [.bootstrap](https://git.sr.ht/~debarchito/.bootstrap)'s
_generate_ templates for Haskell, OCaml, Rust etc. and
[mfctl](https://git.sr.ht/~debarchito/mfctl) to control my
[Arturia MiniFuse 1](https://www.arturia.com/products/audio/minifuse/minifuse-1)
(it was based on a fork I modified to work with [Nix](https://nixos.org)).

> _A tool is as good as the thought (documentation) and care it's creator put
> into it._

You might've noticed that my content is generally very heavy on text; you'd be
correct and this place is no different. That said, d'DG is open-ended so
navigating it can be a bit daunting at first. Thus, I've divided the content
into distinct categories:

- [[The Nursery]] is my experimental blog.
- [[Evergreen Shelf]] holds my literary works viz. poems, stories, et al.
- [[Night Garden]] is my place to get realist when no one calls for it.
