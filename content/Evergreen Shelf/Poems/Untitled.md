> They aren't lovers.\
> They're what's left of Internet history.
>
> All they have left is each other.\
> And so they hold on as the wave of impending doom\
> shatters through each scaffold.
>
> The new era of the Internet washes over them.\
> Leaving them as nothing but a distant memory.
>
> But for all I care, they made this place different.\
> But it's now incoherent, inconceivable.\
> And left "Untitled" on note.
