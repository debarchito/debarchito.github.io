> Ink stains the edges of the page,\
> words stacked like bricks,\
> worlds rising, collapsing,\
> then rising again.
>
> Dreams pressed into sentences,\
> sentences pressed into hours,\
> hours pressed into the weight of waiting.
>
> The desk is still.\
> The chair is still.\
> The air tastes of "almost there."
>
> Then, somewhere a door shuts—\
> too soft to matter,\
> too loud to forget.
