> **Entry: 31st May, 2047**

NOOOOO! How can it arrive so quickly? I have an PE exam; it suuucks! By the way
it's 31st May, 2047; wait didn't I already write that before I started this
entry? Wait, why am I documenting my thoughts to begin with?? Not gonna lie, I
haven't really decided on how I should go about arranging these entries. On the
other hand, I'm just going to treat the first paragraph as internal monologue.
So reader, kindly deal with it.

Summer holidays start right after the exams that end next week. I've completed
my academic preparations; heck I even went ahead and prepared notes for the fall
session. But, in the midst of everything, I've my worst enemy left to face. If I
fail PE, I will be required to stay for remedial classes which are 100% gonna
destroy my summer plans. There is no way I'm gonna let that happen. To add, my
pristine reputation is on the line! (No, seriously). I studied my butt of to get
into **Elo's Federal Institute of Information Engineering** which is one of the
(being humble (=ʘᆽʘ=)∫) best schools in my country, Iotia (pronounced
_i-o-shia_).

Wait, a sec, 31st May...holy shit it's the last day to pay my rent. But, why am
I writing this in the diary again?? Man, it's gonna take some time to get used
to it. Look out for some good character development! Not only is it my first
time writing a diary but I'm doing so with pen and paper. I don't see many
people using those these days, not gonna lie. But, it's kinda cool to use your
fingers to draw stroke at the speed of thought! I don't think I introduced
myself; don't know if it's common courtesy, but I am gonna do it either way.

I'm Kazuto Hona, a 17 year old who likes machines, be it modern or archaic. I
just moved to Elo, the capital city. I don't think I need to add why I moved,
but it's cause I was accepted into EFIIE (or _Efiie~_ if you will). I live alone
in an apartment nearby. I have a part-time job which makes me about enough to
live decently.

> _"What about the tuition?"_

Well, education in Effie is free as it's government funded under the universal
education programs (yea, it has a lot of subsets!). My parents and my younger
sister live in Dai, the second largest city in Iotia after Elo.

Although education is free for me, Elo is still an expensive city. I didn't
think the difference between Dai and Elo would be so huge.

> _"Why don't you ask your parents for support? Are you from a poor family? Bla,
> Bla, Bla..."_

Yeah, no. My family isn't poor; not that rich either but that's not the point ʕ
•ᴥ•ʔ .

> _"Mom, Dad! You don't have to pay for the rent. I can do it all by myself. I
> am your responsible son after all."_

Yeah, that was me in from of my parents. They were so touched by their
"responsible" son's words that they didn't bother to think of a situation where
their "responsible" could've joked! To add salt to the wound, Saya (my younger
sister) went ahead and gave me a good luck charm.

> _"Big brother, don't compromise your diet! Always eat healthy food, don't stay
> awake too late at night, and don't forget to call Saya!"_

I've the cutest little sister in the world, I can't really go back on my words
anymore (￣□￣;). On a serious note, independence isn't all that bad either.

Well, this is enough for one entry. I've my breakfast to prepare and leave for
school afterwards. Writing entries in the morning isn't suitable after all.

Kazuto out.

> `N-E`
