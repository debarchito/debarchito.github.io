> `Narration Start`\
> **Entry: 31st May 2047**

> _"NOOOOO! How can it arrive so quickly? WHY??" \*Screams\*_

I have an PE exam and it suuucks! It's 31st May 2047...wait didn't I already
write that before I started this entry? Wait, why am I documenting my thoughts
to begin with?? \*Sigh\*. I haven't really decided on how I should go about
arranging these entries. I'm just going to treat this paragraph as internal
monologue. So reader, kindly deal with it.

Summer holidays start right after my exams that end next week. I've completed my
academic preparations; heck I even went ahead and prepared materials for fall.
But, in the midst of everything, my worst enemy is still left to face. If I fail
PE, I will be required to stay for remedial classes which are 100% going to
destroy my summer plans. There is no way I'm letting that happen. To add, my
pristine reputation is on the line (no, seriously)! I studied my butt off to get
into **Elo's Federal Institute of Information Engineering** (EFIIE), one of the
(being humble (=ʘᆽʘ=)∫) best schools in my country, Iotia (pronounced
_i-o-shia_).

Wait a sec, today is 31st May...holy sh*t, isn't it the last day of the month??
How can a month go by so fast?? Why am I writing this in this entry again??

\*Sigh\* (I've been sighing a lot, huh). It's going to take some time to get
used to it. Which means, you can look forward to some good character
development! These entries are special because I'm doing so in ink. I don't see
many people writing with their hands these days. It's kind of cool to use your
fingers to draw strokes at the speed of thought!

Oh, I don't think I've introduced myself. I'm not sure if it's a common courtesy
but, at this point, anything goes.

I'm Kazuto Hona, a 17 year-old who likes machines, be it modern or archaic.
Building quirky machines has always been my thing since childhood. Not
everything needs to be useful or solve a problem; the process is often more than
enough. I just moved to Elo, the capital city. I don't think I need to add why I
moved, but it's because I was accepted into EFIIE (or _Efiie~_ if you will). I
live alone in an apartment about 3km away from Effie. I have a part-time job at
a cafe down the road.

> **Reader:** _"A Cafe of all places would've replaced its human staff with
> robots, right?"_

You'd be right, at-least for the most part. Majority of cafes have been
automated. But, this particular cafe, **Te to Te**, has a long-running tradition
of keeping the human element alive. The job makes me enough to live a decent
life.

> **Reader:** _"What about the tuition?"_

Well, education in Effie is free as it's government funded under the universal
education programs (yes, it has a lot of subsets!). My parents and my younger
sister live in Dai, the second largest city in Iotia after Elo. All that said,
Elo is still an expensive city. I didn't think the difference between Dai and
Elo would be so huge. Elo's economy is 12X larger than that of Dai, sitting at
around **¤292 trillion** (¤ pronounced "i-o" is the currency used in Iotia which
is about as strong as €).

> **Reader:** _"Why don't you ask your parents for support? Are you from a poor
> family? Bla, Bla, Bla..."_

Yeah, no. My family isn't poor; not rich either but that's not the point ʕ •ᴥ•ʔ
.

> **Me:** _"Mom, Dad! You don't have to pay for my rent. I can do it all by
> myself. I am your responsible son after all."_

Yeah, that was me in front of my parents. They were so touched by their
"responsible" son's words that they didn't bother to think of a situation where
their "responsible" could've joked! To add salt to the wound, Saya (my younger
sister) went ahead and gave me a good luck charm.

> **Saya:** _"Big brother, don't compromise your diet! Always eat healthy food,
> don't stay awake too late at night, and don't forget to call Saya!"_

I've the cutest little sister in the world, I can't really go back on my words
anymore (￣□￣;). On a serious note, independence isn't all that bad either.

Well, this is enough for one entry. I've my breakfast to prepare and leave for
school afterwards. Writing entries in the morning isn't suitable after all.

Kazuto out.
